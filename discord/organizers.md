🏪 **Welcome, Event Organizers!**
Thank you for running and/or advertising events. You're a big part of what keeps the local scene going! 💜 Your role lets you ping region roles in <#1555056444343197726>, and here are a few things to keep in mind.

**Pinging**
• Please ping the event's region, plus one neighbouring region if it's near the border.
• Ping once when you post the event. The only time to ping again is if the date, time or store changes, or it's cancelled, so nobody shows up on the wrong day.
• Reminders and smaller updates are always welcome, just without the ping.
• @everyone and @here are blocked, and so is any message with more than 3 mentions (that one comes with a timeout, sorry!).

**Posting**
• One post per event, with as many details as you can: date, time, store, format, entry fee, prizing and the Play Hub link.
• If anything changes, please edit your post so it's always up to date. For a big change, also reply to it with a short note (that's where the ping goes).
• Have a quick look that it isn't already posted, so nobody gets pinged twice.
• Want it to count for the league? It has to be on Play Hub. <#1253923057828102175> has the details.

**This channel**
This one's just for us. Ask me anything, share ideas, or check what others have planned so events don't clash. Thanks again! 🙌
