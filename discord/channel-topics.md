# GTA Lorcana — Channel Topics

Paste each into Edit Channel → Overview → Channel Topic (forums: Post Guidelines). Set by hand.
"Read-only" channels are ones @everyone can't post in once Send Messages is off server-wide.

---

## Start here

**#welcome** (read-only)
New here? Read #community-rules and #league-rules, then pick your regions and pings in Channels & Roles.

**#community-rules** (read-only)
Server rules. Breaking them gets a warning, then a timeout, then a ban.

**#discord-announcements** (read-only)
League and server news. Pings @League News. Posts here also appear on gtalorcana.ca.

**#events-announcements** (read-only)
Big events across the GTA: prereleases, Set Championships and special events, posted by staff.

---

## League

**#league-rules** (read-only)
How the league works: seasons, scoring, tiebreakers and the Invitational.

**#results-reporting** (forum)
After an event ends, start a post with its Play Hub link. One post per event. The bot reacts ✅ once it's recorded, or ❌ with the reason if it can't.

**#where-to-play** (read-only)
Stores running weekly Lorcana events around the GTA. Updated automatically by the bot.

**#questions**
Ask about the league, results, roles or the bot. Anyone can answer, and staff check in. To report a problem privately, DM a staff member.

**#hall-of-champions** (read-only)
Invitational champions and top 8s, season by season.

---

## Events

**#store-promos**
Local Lorcana events only, one post per event. Event Organizers can ping the event's region role, plus a neighbouring one. Want the role? DM staff.

**#gta-prereleases** (read-only)
Local prereleases for the upcoming set, as stores list them on Play Hub. Refreshed daily.

**#gta-set-championships** (read-only)
Every Set Championship in the area, refreshed daily through the Set Champs window.

**#ccqs** (read-only)
CCQs within 600 km, six months ahead. Refreshed daily.

---

## Community

**#general**
Talk Lorcana with GTA players. Events go in #store-promos, your own content in #self-promotion.

**#self-promotion**
Your Lorcana streams, videos, art and shops. One post at a time; no spam.

**#etb-discount** (read-only)
Played 3+ league events this season? Press the button for the Enter the Battlefield community discount.

**#roles** (read-only)
The league's rarity roles and how to earn them. Pick region and ping roles in Channels & Roles.
